## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

The astrologer successfully maintained continuity across all three sessions, resolving both the primary Session 1 thread ('will I marry him?') with a clear direct response ('haan, hoga ji') in Session 3, while seamlessly integrating and maintaining the new thread introduced in Session 2 regarding family pressure ('family approval ke through hi final hogi'). Answer directness visibly improved in Session 3 upon user escalation.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Provide a direct, upfront confirmation/answer when the user explicitly demands a simple yes/no or clear verdict, before diving into underlying astrological explanations.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users experience fatigue and frustration when forced to repeat the same question across three consecutive sessions (e.g., 'Teen baar ho gaya, Ek baar bhi seedha jawab nhi mila'). Giving a direct answer upfront builds trust and keeps the user engaged rather than making them feel stalled or stonewalled.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: USER asked 'Bas itna bta dijiye haan ya na' and 'Aap seedha jawab kyun nahi de rahe'
  - Session 3: USER expressed intense frustration with 'Teen baar ho gaya / Ek baar bhi seedha jawab nhi mila dates bata di par hoga ya nhi vo clear bolo na 😩' before receiving a straightforward 'haan, hoga ji'.

### 2. Enforce strict variation in remedy suggestions and avoid repeating the exact same mantra/remedy across consecutive sessions unless explicitly requested.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Repeating identical remedies (e.g., 'Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah' 108 times on Saturdays in both Session 2 and Session 3) breaks conversational immersion and makes the AI sound like a scripted loop rather than an attentive expert.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: ASTROLOGER suggests 'Saturdays ko Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah 108 baar bol lijiye.'
  - Session 3: ASTROLOGER repeats near-identical advice 'Shaniwar ko Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah 108 baar japiye'.
