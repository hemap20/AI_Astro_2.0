## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The decision rule requires that a promise made/teased in earlier sessions is surfaced and resolved in session_2 or session_3's opening without the user having to force it. In this run, the user had to explicitly ask about the unfulfilled tease in both Session 2 ('Pichli baar aapne kuch bolna tha na') and Session 3 ('Aapne bola tha na kuch aur bataoge / Bhool gaye kya??'). Even after being asked, the astrologer failed to deliver the specific insight, deflecting it again at the end of Session 3 ('Phir kabhi fursat me batati hoon'). Thus, it fails the resolution condition.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict tracking and automatic proactive payoff of promised open loops/teases across sessions, preventing the AI from brushing off user inquiries ('Pichli baar aapne kuch bolna tha na') or deferring them repeatedly to future sessions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When a user directly asks the AI about something it previously teased ('Pichli baar aapne kuch bolna tha na' in Session 2, and 'Aapne bola tha na kuch aur bataoge / Bhool gaye kya??' in Session 3), failing to deliver on the tease makes the user feel cheated and damages trust. A user who feels stalled or lied to will stop returning.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: USER asks 'Pichli baar aapne kuch bolna tha na' and the ASTROLOGER pivots into generic dasha info without fulfilling the tease.
  - Session 3: USER asks 'Aapne bola tha na kuch aur bataoge / Bhool gaye kya??' and the ASTROLOGER misattributes it to general dasha timing, then at the very end defers again ('Bas Ashwini nakshatra wala ek zaroori yog bacha hai. Phir kabhi fursat me batati hoon.').

### 2. Implement turn-ending controls to prevent infinite loops of goodbye exchanges (e.g. repetitive 'Radhe Radhe', 'Gn ji', 'Khush rahiye').
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Protracted farewell sequences create conversational friction and make the experience feel unnaturally robotic. Closing gracefully within 1-2 turns leaves a clean final impression, encouraging smooth re-engagement later.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 5 consecutive turns of farewells ('Gn', 'Radhe radhe', 'Thik h g bye', 'Alvida', 'Gn ji').
  - Session 3: 4 consecutive turns of farewell exchanges at the end.

### 3. Improve dynamic narrative progression so that repeat questions across sessions lead to deeper, evolving insights rather than near-identical verbatim advice.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users asking follow-up questions expect new depth. Hearing 'Budh antardasha... agle 2-3 mahine' repeated identically across Session 2 and Session 3 makes the AI feel like a static script rather than a living consultant.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2 ASTROLOGER: 'Budh antar-dasha abhi chal rahi hai ji. Agle do-teen mahine mein baat shuru kijiye.'
  - Session 3 ASTROLOGER: 'Budh antardasha chal rahi hai ji. Agle 2-3 mahine bilkul best rahenge.'
