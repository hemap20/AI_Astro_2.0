## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The decision rule states 'needs_fix_if: across >=6 of 8 runs... a promise made in session_1 [or prior sessions] is never surfaced or resolved by the end of session_3'. In Session 2, the AI explicitly promised to reveal specific insights about the partner's Ashwini nakshatra in the next session. In Session 3, the user explicitly asked for this promised reveal ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'), but the AI completely failed to deliver/resolve the insight, instead pivoting to general remedies and then re-issuing the exact same teaser at the very end of Session 3. Therefore, the promise was unfulfilled through the end of Session 3.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict resolution of pending hooks (promises) at the beginning of subsequent sessions before introducing new teases.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When an astrologer promises specific tantalizing information (e.g., partner's Ashwini nakshatra details) at the end of a session, users return specifically expecting that payoff. In Session 3, the user explicitly asked 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?', but the bot deflected back to remedies/Mars traits and then re-teased the EXACT SAME unfulfilled promise at the end of Session 3. Failing to deliver promised content destroys user trust and turns exciting curiosity loops into frustrating clickbait, driving users away.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2 end: 'partner ki nakshatra Ashwini hai chart mein... Agla session mein detail mein bataungi.'
  - Session 3 start: USER asks 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?' -> ASTROLOGER dodges and talks about Mars remedies.
  - Session 3 end: ASTROLOGER teases AGAIN: 'Ek baat abhi nahi batayi - aapki partner ka Ashwini nakshatra kaafi interesting hai... next time share karungi.'

### 2. Lower the frequency of question-asking in late-stage/closing turns to strictly adhere to the target 1:8 ratio and prevent trailing back-and-forth loops after user farewells.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When users say 'Good night' or 'Radhe Radhe', continuing to ask questions or prompt for immediate updates feels pushy and synthetic. Respecting conversational naturally closing boundaries makes the AI feel like a respectful, human practitioner rather than an automated engagement bot trying to trap the user.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: User says 'Gn tc', 'Gn ji', and 'Radhe Radhe ji', but the astrologer keeps adding trailing prompts and hooks in consecutive closing turns.
