## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The prompt decision rule specifies that if a promise/tease made in Session 1 is never surfaced or resolved without the user having to force it, it requires a fix. In Session 1, the AI teased specific partner details ('partner ke baare mein kuch khaas details... Wo sunna chahoge?'). In Session 2, this was partially addressed via Nakshatra details, but in Session 3 the user explicitly had to force the issue ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge / Bhool gaye kya??'), proving that the forward promise was not smoothly resolved by the AI independently.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce explicit tracking and resolution of unfulfilled dynamic promises across sessions before defaulting to generic recap or open-ended check-ins.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When the user returns expecting a specific reveal promised in a previous turn ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge / Bhool gaye kya??'), failing to immediately deliver damages trust and breaks conversational immersion. Resolving open promises proactively creates strong retention loops and reinforces that the AI is attentively listening.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: Astrologer promises partner details ('partner ke baare mein kuch khaas details... Wo sunna chahoge?') but session ends before resolving.
  - Session 3: User directly calls out forgotten promise: 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge / Bhool gaye kya??'
  - Session 3: Astrologer responds defensively with a summary recap rather than immediately fulfilling the specific tease/promise.

### 2. Strictly enforce memory consistency across sessions for persona and addressing conventions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Switching persona titles (e.g. from 'didi' to 'sir') shatters the personal rapport established in earlier sessions and makes the user feel like they are starting over with a generic chatbot rather than a trusted personal advisor.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: AI introduces as 'Sitara... didi bol sakte ho' and user adapts to calling her 'didi'.
  - Session 3: User opens with 'Hello sir', and the AI fails to re-anchor her persona/addressing preference ('didi'), accepting 'sir' implicitly and re-introducing herself formally ('Main Sitara hoon, aapki Vedic astrologer').

### 3. Prevent post-farewell hook interruptions when the user explicitly says 'Gn tc' or 'bye'.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Attempting to tease information after the user has already initiated a clear sign-off creates friction and feels like desperate stalling rather than natural conversational pacing.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: User says 'Gn tc' and AI interrupts with 'Arre ek second ji! Aapke chart mein partner ke baare mein ek interesting cheez hai...'
