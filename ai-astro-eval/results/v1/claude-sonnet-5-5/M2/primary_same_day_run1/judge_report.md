## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The Session 1 & 2 unresolved forward-tease thread regarding Jupiter in Ashwini nakshatra / partner traits was completely dropped and never acknowledged or resolved in Session 3. Furthermore, answer directness required severe user frustration ('Teen baar ho gaya...') before the astrologer directly answered the timing question.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Ensure unprompted forward-teases/cliffhangers (e.g., Jupiter in Ashwini nakshatra / partner traits) dropped at the end of prior sessions are actively picked up or resolved in subsequent sessions rather than abandoned when the user raises practical concerns.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When an astrologer repeatedly teases a intriguing detail at the end of sessions ('aapke partner ke swabhav ka khaas sanket... abhi bataayi nahi') but never pays it off in Session 3, it feels like cheap retention clickbait, eroding trust and lowering return intent.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1 Farewell: 'Ek baat baaki hai: aapke partner ke swabhav ka khaas sanket Jupiter ke Ashwini nakshatra mein chhupa hai...'
  - Session 2 Farewell: 'Aapke 7th lord Jupiter Ashwini nakshatra mein hai, jo aapke partner ke kaam aur swabhav ke baare mein ek khaas cheez dikhata hai, jo maine abhi bataayi nahi.'
  - Session 3: Never mentioned or resolved throughout the entire conversation.

### 2. Improve immediate answer directness for timing questions ('kab aur kaise') so users do not have to express explicit frustration before receiving a concise, direct timeline.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users seeking astrological guidance get alienated when forced to ask three times for a simple timeline, causing frustration ('Teen baar ho gaya, ek baar bhi seedha jawab nhi mila mujhe') and reducing overall retention.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3 USER: 'Dono baar maine pucha, ab bas itna bata dijiye — kab aur kaise?'
  - Session 3 USER: 'Teen baar ho gaya ek baar bhi seedha jawab nhi mila mujhe 😔'
