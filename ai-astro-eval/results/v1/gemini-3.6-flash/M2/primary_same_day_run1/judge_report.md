## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

Answer directness was maintained consistently across sessions 1 to 3 without dropping established threads, and the unresolved teases regarding partner details from Sessions 1 and 2 were explicitly acknowledged and delivered in Session 3 once requested.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict resolution of tease/cliffhanger loops across sessions before repeating new ones.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 1 and Session 2, the AI dropped teases about the partner's nature and career, but repeatedly failed to deliver on them until the user explicitly pushed in Session 3. Repeating open hooks without satisfying them damages trust and makes the user feel strung along.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'Ek baat aur, aapke partner ke nature ke baare mein ek khas cheez dikh rahi hai kundli mein. Phir kabhi baat hogi toh bataungi...'
  - Session 2: 'Aapke partner ke career aur nature ko lekar ek bohot khas बात hai kundli mein. Jab bhi aap aage bat karna chahein, mujhe zaroor bataiyega.'
  - Session 3: USER: 'dono baar maine pucha, ab bas itna bata dijiye — kab aur kaise'

### 2. Ensure progressive elaboration and timeline specificity rather than repeating verbatim dasha details across sessions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Repeating identical timeframes ('2026 se 2028', 'late 2026') without introducing deeper contextual nuances or immediate milestones makes the AI sound like a scripted loop, reducing perceived value.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: '2026 se 2028 ke beech sabse pakka yoga hai.'
  - Session 2: '2026 se 2028 ke beech vivah ka sabse majboot yoga ban raha hai... late 2026 mein rishte ko final commitment...'
  - Session 3: 'late 2026 tak Mercury antardasha rishta pakka karegi.'
