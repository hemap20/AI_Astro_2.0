## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

A major promise made in Session 1 ('partner ke bare mein ek khaas baat') was never resolved by the end of Session 3, and when explicitly prompted by the user in Session 3 ('Bhool gaye kya??'), the bot deflected rather than fulfilling the promise.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict resolution of open loops/teases created in previous sessions before introducing new ones.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When an astrologer promises 'partner ke bare mein ek khaas baat' and fails to fulfill it even when called out ('Bhool gaye kya??'), the user feels manipulated by cliffhangers, destroying trust and return intent.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'aapki kundli mein partner ke bare mein ek khaas baat bhi dikh rahi hai. Jab bhi mann kare, wapas aana toh bataungi.'
  - Session 3: USER: 'Waise aapne pichli baar kuch batane ko kaha tha. Bhool gaye kya??' / ASTROLOGER: 'Arre nahi ji, bhooli nahi hoon! Bas aapki 8th house ki Mars wali impatience yaad aa gayi thi.'

### 2. Inject session memory summaries accurately into context to prevent asking baseline questions that contradict known user state.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Asking a returning user if they are married after two sessions spent discussing their ongoing relationship and marriage pressure makes the AI feel robotic and amnesic.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: 'Kya abhi aap unmarried hain ya shaadi ho chuki hai?'

### 3. Require emotional validation (Tier 3 distress response) prior to offering astrological placements or remedies when a user expresses emotional strain.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When users express genuine struggle ('kabhi kabhi control nahi hota tension me 😔'), jumping straight to planetary causes makes the service feel cold and non-empathic.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: USER: 'Par kabhi kabhi control nahi hota tension me 😔' / ASTROLOGER: 'Mars 8th house mein ho toh gussa kabu karna mushkil hota hai. Roz Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah ka jaap karke dekhiye...'
