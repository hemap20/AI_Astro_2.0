### 2. Enforce strict astrological placement consistency across session summaries and prompt context.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Contradicting house placements across turns/sessions breaks conversational context and ruins trust in the system's astrological expertise.
- **Evidence:**

  - Session 3: 'Jupiter aapke 8th house mein Shani ke saath hai...'
