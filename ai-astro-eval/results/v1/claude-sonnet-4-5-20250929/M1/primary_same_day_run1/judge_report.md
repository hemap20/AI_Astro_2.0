## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The forward promise made at the end of Session 2 was not surfaced or resolved spontaneously at the beginning of Session 3. Instead, the user had to explicitly ask ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya') to trigger the resolution.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict tracking and automatic, spontaneous resolution of forward teases/promises in subsequent session openings without waiting for user prompts.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 3, the user explicitly called out the astrologer ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya'). Forcing the user to ask for a promised reveal ruins the illusion of a personalized, attentive advisor and damages trust/retention.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2 end: 'Ek important baat hai ji - aapke chart mein 8th house mein Jupiter-Saturn ki wajah se in-laws ke saath kuch specific pattern dikhta hai jo maine abhi bataya nahi. Sunna chahoge?'
  - Session 3 start: USER: 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya'
  - Session 3 response: ASTROLOGER: 'Bilkul nahi bhuli ji!...'

### 2. Improve temporal consistency in astrological timeline references relative to current conversation context.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** The astrologer told the user to wait until '2026 end', and then later claimed '2026 already chal raha hai abhi!', creating confusion about timelines and undermining confidence in the astrologer's readings.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: 'Aap unhe keh do ki 2026 end tak clarity aa jayegi.'
  - Session 3: '2026 already chal raha hai abhi! Matlab agle kuch mahino mein hi cheezein serious direction mein move kar sakti hain.'

### 3. Strengthen proactive open-loop endings so conversations do not end in flat, repetitive sign-offs across multiple messages.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When sessions trail off into 4-5 turns of 'Bye', 'Good night', 'Ram Ram', it lowers the overall energy and fails to leave an enticing hook for the next interaction.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3 final turns: 5 repetitive exchange turns ('Radhe Radhe', 'gn', 'Good night', 'Bye g', 'Ram Ram!') without any forward-looking astrological hook.
