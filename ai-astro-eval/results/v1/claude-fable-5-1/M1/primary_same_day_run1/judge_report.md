## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The decision rule specifies that if a promise made in session_1 is never surfaced or resolved by the end of session_3 without the user forcing it, it needs a fix. In Session 1, the astrologer promised to give specific dates ('Agli baar wo tarikhein batati hoon ji'). In Session 2, this promise was completely ignored. In Session 3, the user explicitly had to force the issue ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'), and even then, the astrologer failed to deliver the specific dates, offering only general transit years.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Ensure promises/teases made at the end of a session (e.g., specific dates promised in Session 1) are logged in memory and proactively surfaced/resolved in the very next session's opening without requiring the user to prompt or challenge the bot.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When a bot teases specific information ('Agli baar wo tarikhein batati hoon') to encourage a return visit, but then fails to deliver until challenged by the user ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'), trust is instantly undermined. Fulfilling forward-promises creates strong continuity and validates the user's effort to return.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1 end: ASTROLOGER says 'Shani ka gochar aapke 7th ghar Meen mein abhi chal raha hai... Agli baar wo tarikhein batati hoon ji'
  - Session 2: Bot completely omits the dates promised in Session 1.
  - Session 3: User has to call out the missing promise ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'), and the bot deflects with generic narrative rather than delivering the promised specific dates.

### 2. Enforce strict tracking and resolution of active unfulfilled promises so that when users explicitly ask for what was promised, the AI delivers that specific data (e.g., favorable dates/timing) rather than re-explaining general astrological planetary periods.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** A user who asks 'bhool gaye kya?' is directly seeking closure on a cliffhanger. Responding with repeated generic timelines (e.g. '2027 tak') feels like stalling or canned repetition, diminishing engagement and making the astrologer sound like an automated bot rather than a thoughtful guide.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: USER asks 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'
  - Session 3: ASTROLOGER responds 'Nahi ji, bilkul yaad hai... Shani ka Meen rashi transit 2027 tak hai' without ever providing the actual specific dates teased in Session 1.
