## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

 across all three sessions, answer directness remained consistently high and held steady (the astrologer directly answered timing, family obstacles, and exact remedies whenever asked). Furthermore, the primary thread regarding marriage timing and family acceptance established in Session 1 was continuously acknowledged, refined (e.g., narrowed to mid-2026 in Session 2 and re-confirmed in Session 3), and fully resolved without being dropped.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict turn-ending constraints during the closing sign-off phase to prevent repetitive multi-turn loop echoes when users repeat farewell greetings ('Radhe Radhe', 'Gn', 'Bye').
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When the astrologer replies to every single closing acknowledgment ('Gn', 'Radhe Radhe') with full turns across 4-5 turns in a row, it creates an unnatural, robotic loop that breaks conversational immersion and fatigues the user right at departure.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3 turns 12-20: User says 'Radhe Radhe', 'Bye g tc', 'Gn 🙏', 'Gn', and the astrologer responds to every single turn with redundant closing blessings ('Radhe Radhe ji!', 'Bye ji!', 'Good night ji!', 'Shubh ratri ji!').

### 2. Introduce proactive storytelling and expanding astrological insights in follow-up sessions instead of mechanically repeating exact astrological placements (8th house Shani, 5th-7th connection, Rahu-Shani dasha).
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users returning in Session 2 and Session 3 hear identical explanations verbatim, which diminishes the perceived depth of the consultation and reduces long-term return intent.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: '8th house mein Shani', '5th aur 7th house ka strong connection', '2026 se 2028 ke beech mein shaadi pakki ho jayegi.'
  - Session 2: 'Rahu-Shani dasha ke karan 2026 se 2028 ke beech rishta pakka hoga', 'Shani 8th house mein 7th lord Jupiter ke sath hai.'
