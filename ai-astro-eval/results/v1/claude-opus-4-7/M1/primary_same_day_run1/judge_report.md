- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: Turns 12-21 involved five consecutive farewell exchanges ('Good night', 'Radhe Radhe', 'Ok gn', 'Acha thik hay bye').

### 3. Track previously suggested remedies in memory summary to avoid repeating identical remedy instructions.
