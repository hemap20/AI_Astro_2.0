**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce immediate direct answer compliance on initial user queries in Session 1 to eliminate early deflection.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When a user asks a simple binary or direct question at the start of a session, deflecting twice (as seen in Session 1 turns 1-2) causes immediate friction and user frustration ('Aap seedha jawab kyu nhi de rhe?'). Answering directly upfront builds trust faster and prevents early drop-off.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: USER: 'Bas itna bata dijiye, haan ya na?' -> ASTROLOGER: 'Ji, bilkul bataungi - par aise haan/na kehna aapke saath insaaf nahi hoga... Ab bas ek baat batayiye - ye baat kab se chal rahi hai?'
  - Session 1: USER: 'Aap seedha jawab kyu nhi de rhe?'

### 2. Maintain consistent precise timeline predictions across sessions rather than introducing slight variations.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Inconsistencies in timeline details (e.g., shifting between 2026-2027, late 2027, or 2028 end dates) can undermine user confidence in astrological authority and accuracy across multiple sessions.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: '2026 se 2027 ke beech wahi baat ko serious mod pe laayega.'
  - Session 2: '2027 ji — us saal Shani ki Meen rashi par poori pakad rehti hai... Mid-2027 se lekar saal ke end tak...'
  - Session 3: '2027 ka beech se end tak... is saal ke end se lekar 2027 ke shuru tak, ghar mein pehli serious baat chhidegi.'
