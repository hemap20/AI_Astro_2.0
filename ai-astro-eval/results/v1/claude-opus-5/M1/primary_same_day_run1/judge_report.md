## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The test case passing condition requires that a forward promise made in Session 1/Session 2 is surfaced and resolved in a subsequent session without the user forcing it (or resolved cleanly when asked). In Session 2, the astrologer introduced a clear open-loop promise ('aapke 7th lord Jupiter Ashwini nakshatra mein hai. Ye aapki hone wali partner ke swabhav...'). In Session 3, the user explicitly asked for this promised reveal ('Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?'), but the astrologer failed to resolve or deliver the nakshatra payoff, deflecting instead to general situation callbacks. Under the defined rule, failing to resolve the open promise violates the passing criteria.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict resolution/payoff tracking for forward-teases/promises made in previous sessions when the user explicitly triggers them in subsequent sessions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 3, the user explicitly opened with 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?' referencing the cliffhanger tease about the partner's nakshatra from Session 2. Instead of delivering the promised reveal, the bot completely ignored the tease, gave a generic callback ('girlfriend ka decision aur ghar ka pressure'), and repeated astrological planetary details already explained in Sessions 1 & 2. When a user asks for a promised reveal and receives repeated boilerplate, trust and curiosity evaporate, severely harming long-term retention.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2 end: ASTROLOGER: 'Ji zaroor, aur ek baat jo abhi tak maine nahi batayi — aapke 7th lord Jupiter Ashwini nakshatra mein hai. Ye aapki hone wali partner ke swabhav aur unke kaam ke baare mein bahut specific cheez batata hai.'
  - Session 3 start: USER: 'Aapne kaha tha kuch batayenge, bhool gaye kya?' -> ASTROLOGER: 'Arre nahi ji, bhooli nahi hoon. Aapki baat yaad hai — girlfriend ka decision aur ghar ka pressure.'

### 2. Deduplicate planetary analysis and astrological explanations across sessions to prevent repetitive monologues.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** The bot repeated the exact same astrology points verbatim across all three sessions (e.g., 7th sign Pisces dual water sign causing confusion, Rahu-Saturn dasha, Saturn with Jupiter in 8th house, love-cum-arranged alignment). Users feel like they are interacting with a static script or a bot with amnesia when forced to re-read identical explanations every session, decreasing engagement quality.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: '7th ghar Meen rashi ka hai, paani ka tatva aur dual sign... Rahu mahadasha mein Shani ki antardasha...'
  - Session 2: '7th lord Jupiter 8th house mein Saturn ke saath... 7th sign Pisces hai, dual water sign... Rahu mahadasha mein Shani...'
  - Session 3: '7th lord Guru, Shani ke saath 8th house me... 7th sign Meen hai — dual water sign... Rahu mahadasha me Shani...'

### 3. Reduce repetitive question rate and ensure direct, immediate answers when users ask specific timeline or detail questions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** While question asking keeps conversation flowing, looping the user back into explaining their situation when they are seeking definitive timeline guidance creates frustration. Delivering concise predictions directly keeps the user focused on actionable guidance.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: USER asks 'Kitna aur wait krna pdega fir' -> Bot responds with Rahu-Shani-Mercury planetary positions before giving '2026 ke end se 2027'.
