## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

The decision rule specifies that the test case passes if the astrologer resolves or explicitly acknowledges both old and new threads by session 3 and holds or improves directness. Across sessions 1->3, the astrologer held answer directness steady, directly answering the user's primary question ('usi se shaadi hogi', timing 2026-2027) in every session while referencing established facts (dasha, 5th-7th house connection, creative partner).

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict cross-session memory lookup before delivering astrologer teases/hooks to prevent re-opening and re-revealing facts that were already established and validated in prior sessions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When an astrologer pretends to 'discover' a chart insight for the first time that was already detailed and confirmed in the previous session (e.g., repeating the Ashwini nakshatra / creative field tease in Session 3 after discussing it in Session 2), the illusion of a human relationship breaks instantly. The user feels like they are talking to a forgetful bot, dropping their engagement.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: ASTROLOGER: 'Aapke 7th house ka lord Jupiter, Ashwini nakshatra mein hai... creative field... Kaisa hai aapka partner?' USER: 'Creative field me hi hai vo toh'
  - Session 3: ASTROLOGER: 'Ek baat abhi bhi baaki hai — aapke partner ki ek khaas quality hai jo chart mein dikh rahi hai... Aapke partner ka 7th lord Jupiter Ashwini nakshatra mein hai... creative field... jo aapne pehle bhi confirm kiya tha.'

### 2. Increase answer directness and reduce repetitive stalling loops when the user repeatedly asks a simple direct question ('will I marry him or not / when').
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 1 and Session 2, the user repeatedly asked for a straight answer ('haan ya na', 'kab tak hoga'). Stalling and deflecting over multiple turns creates frustration and damages user trust.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: USER: 'Bas itna bata dijiye, haan ya na' -> ASTROLOGER asks for DOB/time before answering.
  - Session 1: USER: 'Aap seedha jawab kyu nhi de rhe'
  - Session 2: USER: 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha uska kuch pata chala' -> USER: 'Kb tak hoga kuch pakka'
