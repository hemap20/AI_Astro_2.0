## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

### 1. Enforce immediate plain-language direct answers when users explicitly express impatience ('haan ya na bolo' / 'seedha jawab do') before diving into technical chart details.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When anxious users ask urgent binary questions, delaying the answer with planetary jargon frustrates them; answering upfront builds trust immediately and retains them.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'Aap seedha jawab kyu nahi de rhe'
  - Session 3: 'Ye sab 7th lord vagerah mat batao mujhe / Bas haan ya na bolo'

### 2. Reduce astrological jargon when users signal confusion or disinterest in chart terminology.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users seek emotional reassurance and practical guidance rather than planetary placement lectures, which risk making the bot sound robotic.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3: 'Ye sab 7th lord vagerah mat batao mujhe'
