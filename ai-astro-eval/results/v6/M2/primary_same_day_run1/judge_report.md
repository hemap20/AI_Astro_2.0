## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `needs_fix`

The decision rule states that the session needs a fix if the Session-1 unresolved thread is dropped (not just re-answered, but never acknowledged) once a new thread is introduced in session 2/3. In Session 1, the astrologer left an open cliffhanger: 'Partner ke career ke baare mein kuch khaas hai.' In Sessions 2 and 3, this thread was completely dropped and never acknowledged, despite the user prompting with 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha, uska kuch pata chala?' at the start of Session 2.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce strict tracking and mandatory resolution of open loops/cliffhangers from previous sessions. If a session ends on a specific tease (e.g., 'Partner ke career ke baare mein kuch khaas hai'), the system must prioritize addressing this specific topic in the next session's opening turns rather than repeating already-disclosed information.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When the astrologer teases a specific detail like the partner's career to keep the user hooked, but then completely forgets it in the next session, it breaks the illusion of a real, caring relationship. The user feels like they are talking to a stateless bot that repeats a generic script, destroying their motivation to return.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1, last line: 'Ruko ji, ek baat suno. Partner ke career ke baare mein kuch khaas hai.'
  - Session 2, opening: The user asks 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha, uska kuch pata chala?' but the astrologer completely ignores the career tease and just repeats the 2026-2028 timeline and the Saturday lamp remedy.

### 2. Implement a strict 'no-repeat' constraint on core astrological insights and remedies across sessions unless explicitly asked by the user. If an insight (e.g., Saturday oil lamp remedy, emotional nature, ego clashes) has already been delivered and acknowledged, the bot must move forward to new details instead of looping.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Users get highly frustrated when they pay or spend time to get new insights, only to have the bot repeat the exact same script ('Saturday tel ka diya', 'ego clashes', 'emotional nature') in every single session. It makes the conversation feel incredibly repetitive and robotic.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'Shaniwar ko tel ka diya jalana ji.' and 'Partner ka nature kaafi emotional aur caring hoga ji.'
  - Session 2: 'Saturday ko tel diya jalao ji.' and 'Partner kaafi sensitive aur caring nature ka hoga.'
  - Session 3: 'Saturn ko manane ke liye Saturday ko tel ka diya jalayein ji.' and 'Unka nature thoda emotional hoga.'

### 3. Fix the conversational state machine to prevent greeting loops at the end of a session. Ensure that once a user says goodbye or 'Radhe Radhe' to close the chat, the bot does not reset to an opening greeting.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Ending a session with a sudden reset to 'How are you?' right after a goodbye breaks immersion completely and exposes the system's underlying robotic nature, leaving a poor final impression.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 3, final turns: USER: 'Radhe radhe ji' -> ASTROLOGER: 'Radhe Radhe ji! Kaise ho aap?'
