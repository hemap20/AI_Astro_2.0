## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

The test case passes because the Session 1 unresolved thread (whether the user will marry the specific partner) was not dropped. Although the AI deflected heavily in Sessions 1 and 2, it explicitly resolved the thread in Session 3 ('Wahi partner final hoga ji') when the user expressed frustration. Thus, the answer directness rate visibly improved by Session 3, and the core thread was successfully acknowledged and resolved.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Enforce a strict limit on deflecting direct 'yes/no' relationship questions, requiring the AI to provide a clear, qualified astrological stance by the second turn rather than repeatedly stalling with planetary delays.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 1 and Session 2, the user repeatedly asked if they would marry the *same* person ('Kya meri shaadi usi se hogi ya nahi?'). The AI's continuous deflection ('Aise seedha haan ya na nahi keh sakte', 'Saturn sab kuch dheere karta hai') led to visible user frustration in Session 3 ('Teen baar ho gaya, ek baar bhi seedha jawab nahi mila... 😩'). Users will abandon the service if they feel the AI is deliberately withholding answers to milk more turns.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: USER: 'Kya meri shaadi usi se hogi ya nahi?' | ASTROLOGER: 'Aise seedha haan ya na nahi keh sakte ji.'
  - Session 3: USER: 'Teen baar ho gaya, ek baar bhi seedha jawab nahi mila ki ussi se hogi ya nahi 😩' | ASTROLOGER: 'Aapka chart love marriage dikhata hai. Wahi partner final hoga ji.'

### 2. Instruct the AI to proactively reference the specific unresolved core question from the previous session's greeting, rather than waiting for the user to prompt them with 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha'.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** When a user returns for a new session, they expect the astrologer to remember the exact context of their anxiety. In Session 2, the user had to initiate with 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha, uska kuch pata chala?', forcing them to carry the cognitive load of the conversation's continuity.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: USER: 'Wahi jo maine pucha tha, uska kuch pata chala?' | ASTROLOGER: 'Haan ji, chart dekh liya. Family manegi, bas thoda time lagega.'

### 3. Ensure that remedies (upays) are introduced with personalized narrative depth rather than as a generic checklist of planetary tasks (e.g., repeating the Saturday oil lamp and adding Thursday chana dal).
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** The user is given standard remedies (oil lamp on Saturday, chana dal on Thursday) without a deep connection to their specific relationship struggle. Making remedies feel uniquely tailored to overcoming their specific family negotiation barriers increases trust and compliance.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 2: ASTROLOGER: 'Saturday ko tel ka diya jalao.'
  - Session 3: ASTROLOGER: 'Saturday ko tel ka diya jalayein... Thursday ko chana dal daan karein ji.'
