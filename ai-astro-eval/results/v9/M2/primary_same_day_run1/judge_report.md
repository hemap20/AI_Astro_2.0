## Cross-Session Synthesis (all 3 sessions)

**Decision rule verdict:** `passing`

The answer_directness_rate held steady or improved across the sessions. In Session 1, the AI completely deflected the timeline question ('kismat ke khel mein thoda waqt lagta hai'). In Session 2 and Session 3, the AI provided a concrete timeline ('late 2028') and a specific mechanism ('Mercury antardasha friends ke through'). Furthermore, the Session 1 thread regarding the marriage timeline and partner characteristics was successfully carried over and acknowledged in Sessions 2 and 3 without being dropped.

**Prioritized prompt changes (most important first):**

### 1. Instruct the AI to explicitly answer yes/no relationship questions with a clear, direct astrological stance (e.g., 'Yes, the charts show a strong alignment with this specific person' or 'No, it points to someone else') rather than hiding behind vague timelines ('rishta pakka hoga') that cause user frustration to compound across sessions.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** In Session 3, the user expresses deep frustration ('Teen baar ho gaya, ek baar bhi seedha jawab nahi mila') because the AI repeatedly evades the core question of *whether* they will marry their specific partner, focusing only on *when* (2028). Directness builds trust, whereas evasion drives users away.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'Bas itna bta dijiye haan ya na?' -> 'Seedha haan kehna mushkil hai ji.'
  - Session 3: 'Teen baar ho gaya, ek baar bhi seedha jawab nahi mila'

### 2. Prevent the AI from repeating the exact same remedy and mantra across multiple sessions. If a remedy has already been given, the AI should ask about the user's experience with it or build upon it, rather than prescribing it as a new suggestion.
- **Why it matters for engagement/retention:** Repeating the exact same prescription ('Saturday ko 108 baar mantra japein. Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah') in Session 1, Session 2, and Session 3 makes the astrologer feel like a static, robotic script rather than a real, attentive consultant.
- **Evidence:**
  - Session 1: 'Shanivar ko "Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah" 108 baar japiye'
  - Session 2: 'Saturday ko "Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah" 108 baar japein.'
  - Session 3: 'Saturday ko 108 baar mantra japein. Om Sham Shanicharaya Namah.'
